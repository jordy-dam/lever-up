# Prompt spierkaart (Gemini)

Twee plaatjes, apart genereren: eerst voorkant, dan achterkant met "zelfde stijl, pose en kadrering".
Sla op als `assets/muscles/front.png` en `assets/muscles/back.png`.

## Voorkant

Flat 2D vector illustration of a muscular male body, full body, front view, standing straight with arms slightly away from the body, palms forward. Headless is fine, no face. Plain white background. Muted dark navy and slate-grey colour palette, flat colours, no gradients, no shadows, no gloss. Each muscle group is ONE separate flat-coloured shape, separated from its neighbours by a clear thin white gap, so every group is a closed shape. Visible groups: chest, front shoulders, biceps, forearms, abs (one block, no individual six-pack segments), obliques (side abs), hip flexors, quads (upper front legs), calves. Use a slightly different shade of the same colour for each group so they can be told apart. Simple, clean, no text, no labels, no arrows. Centred with generous margin, 3:4 portrait, at least 2000 px high.

## Achterkant

Same image, same style, same pose, same framing and same body size, but back view. Visible groups: rear shoulders, triceps, forearms, upper back (traps and between the shoulder blades), lats, lower back, glutes, hamstrings, calves. Each group ONE separate flat shape with a clear white gap around it.

## Controle voor je ze stuurt

- Elke spiergroep is één gesloten vlak (geen losse streepjes of vezels erin).
- Voor- en achterkant staan op dezelfde plek en even groot.
- Geen tekst, geen pijlen, geen schaduw of verloop.
